Capture it right

  • Camera behind you, on the target line, at roughly hand height. This is the only supported angle.
  • 240fps slo-mo if your phone supports it, landscape, braced so it doesn't move mid-clip.
  • Full body in frame, with headroom for the club at the top of the swing.
  • Hit as many balls as you like in one clip -- swings are split out automatically.
